What will you learn?

Learning outcomes

By the end of this tutorial, you should be able to:

  1. Diagnose why an MDP is difficult to solve

    Identify challenges in the state space, decisions, and evaluation of future consequences.

  2. Explain how adaptation can add value

    Use the construct–optimize–refine cycle to focus approximation effort where it can improve decisions.

  3. Assess the value of adaptation

    Less user effort, more efficient computation, better decisions.

We hope this foundation helps you explore adaptation’s value in your own MDP research and applications.
