# Solver logos used on slide 9

Retrieved on October 24, 2026; kept locally for offline presentation.

- `gurobi.svg`: dark Gurobi logo displayed by the official SCIP site, downloaded from https://scipopt.org/images/GurobiLogo_Black-1-1-1.svg. The light-background variant is used without changing its geometry or colors.
- `hexaly.svg`: official Hexaly wordmark, downloaded from https://www.hexaly.com/wp-content/themes/hexaly/assets/images/hexaly-logo-orange.svg.
- `scip.png`: official SCIP Optimization Suite mascot, downloaded from https://scipopt.org/images/newscippy.png; its white artwork is displayed over a dark circular backing and accompanied by the text “SCIP” for legibility at slide size.

The logos identify examples of solvers. The solution illustration is an independently typeset result for the slide's mathematical program, not a screenshot or a recorded run of any of these products.
